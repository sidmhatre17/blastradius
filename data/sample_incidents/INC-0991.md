# INC-0991 — Shared HttpClient retry amplification outage

## Summary
A change to `packages/common/http_client.py` increased default retries and shortened timeouts.
Every importer (gateway, billing worker, notify sender, auth validate) simultaneously amplified load.

## Impact
- Card processor and ledger latency collapse
- Cascading timeouts across money path
- SEV-1 lasting 41 minutes

## Root cause
Shared library defaults are a multi-service blast radius. Retry/backoff knobs looked "local" but were imported by ≥6 call sites.

## Fix
Reverted default retries to 2, introduced per-client overrides, and required review from platform + money teams for `packages/common/**` changes.

## Lessons
Diffs touching `packages/common/http_client.py` should be treated as critical until proven otherwise.
