# INC-0888 — Billing retry exhaustion without DLQ

## Summary
`services/billing_worker/retry.py` exhausted retries on processor timeouts but only emitted a best-effort notify event.
Charges were left in limbo with no dead-letter queue.

## Impact
- Duplicate capture attempts after manual replay
- Finance reconciliation mismatch
- On-call pages from notify retry_exhausted templates

## Root cause
Retry policy doubled delay aggressively while max_attempts stayed high; exhaustion path lacked durable DLQ write.

## Fix
Capped attempts, persisted exhausted jobs, and made notify emission secondary to DLQ durability.

## Lessons
Changes to billing retry semantics need settlement replay tests before merge.
