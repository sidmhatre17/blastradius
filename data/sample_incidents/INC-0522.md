# INC-0522 — Ledger post after partial capture

## Summary
`services/billing_worker/worker.py` wrote ledger entries even when processor capture returned partial approval.
Books showed settled for incomplete captures.

## Impact
- Reconciliation break
- Risk review freeze on settlements

## Root cause
Missing status check on capture payload before ledger post.

## Fix
Required `capture.status == approved` before ledger write; added worker integration test.
